Privacy and terms

Last updated 18 September 2026. Written in plain words for a personal project; this is not legal advice.

What is stored

Shiori runs on your own computer. Everything it keeps stays there: your AniList token, watch history, settings, downloaded episodes and chapters, and logs. The login token lives in your browser. There are no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and nothing is sold or shared.

What leaves your computer

AniList receives your list updates when you connect it. The sources you install receive the searches and requests needed to fetch episodes and chapters, and see your computer's IP address. Torrent peers see your IP address while you download or seed; use a VPN or a debrid service if that matters where you live. Each of those services has its own privacy policy.

This website

This site is static pages. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Fonts, icons and the animation library load from Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which see your IP address like any website does.

Your responsibility

Shiori is a personal, non-commercial, self-hosted build of the open-source Seanime media server. It hosts, uploads and stores no media.
